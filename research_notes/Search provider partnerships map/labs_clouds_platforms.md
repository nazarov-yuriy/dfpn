## KQ1: Which labs/clouds have a documented default or built-in third-party search backend?

### Takeaway
Only three first-party platform defaults name a third party outright: Anthropic → Brave (via subprocessor disclosure, not docs), OpenRouter → Exa (fallback default for non-native models), and Cloudflare → Ceramic.ai (default provider of its reseller API).
